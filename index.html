About Us

The BU Beekeeping Club is a student organization dedicated to promoting the health of pollinators, hands-on beekeeping education, and sustainable practices on and around campus. Whether you're an experienced apiarist or have never seen the inside of a hive, all buzz levels are welcome!

Our Mission

We're bringing beekeeping back to BU. Our goal is to help students learn about honeybees and the role they play in our environment, through hands-on events and sustainable practices that make campus a little more bee-friendly.

Our Hives

We keep two honeybee hives in the rear garden behind 164-168 Bay State Road. They're home to Pol-Line honeybees, a gentle, calm breed that does well on an urban campus.

Living Near the Hives

Honeybees are foragers. They're focused on flowers and pollen, not people or food, and won't sting unless they feel threatened. Stay calm, avoid swatting, and feel free to reach out any time with questions.
